## About

**NK Bag House** is a bulk bag manufacturing business owned by **S. Elayaraja, Advocate**, providing bags for orders ranging from **10 pieces to 100,000 pieces**.

The NK Bag House web platform is being developed to bring the business online through a polished, user-friendly digital experience and help the business reach a wider audience.

## Product Range

The website will organise the communicated product range into two easy-to-browse groups:

**Manufactured bag categories**
- School Bags
- Travel Bags
- General Bags

**Leather products**
- Leather Bags
- Leather Wallets
- Leather Keychains
- Leather Card Holders
- Leather Belts

The business's stated positioning is affordable pricing with quality-focused options. Product-level availability, specifications, pricing, and whether each leather item is manufactured in-house or sourced/supplied will be confirmed before publication.

## Purpose

The platform is designed to make it easier for customers to:

- Explore School Bags, Travel Bags, and General Bags
- Browse leather bags, wallets, keychains, card holders, and belts
- Understand verified product options and specifications
- Submit bulk requirements
- Request custom requirements
- Connect directly with NK Bag House

The website will provide a responsive experience across desktop and mobile devices while maintaining a maintainable foundation for future development.

The project is being developed progressively from customer requirements and UI direction to a complete production-ready web application.

**Requirements → UX/UI → Frontend → Enquiry Workflow → Backend & Data → Testing → Deployment**

### Current Technology

The initial frontend foundation has been created with:

- Next.js 16
- React
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS

Backend, database, storage, and hosting decisions will be finalized when the customer requirements and enquiry workflow are confirmed.

Retail websites such as Peter England and Allen Solly are visual-quality references only; NK Bag House will have its own identity and B2B-focused experience.
