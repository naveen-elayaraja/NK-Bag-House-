## About

**NK Bag House** is a bulk bag manufacturing business owned by **S. Elayaraja, Advocate**, providing bags for orders ranging from **10 pieces to 100,000 pieces**.

The NK Bag House web platform is being developed to bring the business online through a clean, user-friendly digital experience and help the business reach a wider audience.

## Purpose

The platform is designed to make it easier for customers to:

- Explore available bag products
- Understand product options
- Submit bulk requirements
- Request custom requirements
- Connect directly with NK Bag House

The website will provide a responsive experience across desktop and mobile devices while maintaining a scalable foundation for future development.
