# NK Bag House — Content and Media Guidelines

## 1. Content goals

Website content should help a customer understand the product, assess whether it may suit their requirement, and contact the business. Use concise, specific, factual language.

- Prefer plain English and familiar industry terminology.
- Explain technical terms when a general buyer may not know them.
- Use headings, short paragraphs, and specification lists.
- Keep calls to action consistent, such as "Explore Products" and "Request a Quote".
- Avoid unsupported superlatives, guarantees, certifications, or delivery promises.
- Do not publish placeholder content as if it were verified business information.

## 2. Product information

Each product entry should have:
- Approved product name and category.
- A short summary.
- Verified specifications with units.
- Available options and customisation details.
- Minimum order quantity, if product-specific and approved.
- Approved product photographs.
- A route to enquire about that specific product.

If a specification is unknown, leave it unpublished until the business verifies it. Do not infer safe working load, material grade, dimensions, compliance, or other technical claims from a photograph.

## 3. Photography direction

The preferred visual style is clean, confident, and consistent:

- Use original photographs of actual NK Bag House products wherever possible.
- Capture a clean hero image with enough space for readable text.
- Photograph products from consistent angles and distances.
- Include close-up details only when they explain a meaningful product feature.
- Show manufacturing processes or facilities only when the business approves the images and descriptions.
- Use natural, balanced lighting and uncluttered backgrounds.
- Keep important product details inside the crop-safe area.
- Obtain permission for any customer, employee, supplier, or third-party material shown.

## 4. Suggested image roles

| Image role | Suggested treatment | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage hero | Wide, high-quality product or manufacturing scene | Awaiting approved image |
| Product category | Consistent landscape or square crop | Awaiting catalogue |
| Product detail | Clear product view plus useful detail views | Awaiting catalogue |
| Capability section | Genuine facility or process image | Optional; approval required |
| About page | Approved business or team photograph | Optional |

These are content-planning suggestions, not a request to use stock images or competitor imagery.

## 5. Image optimisation and accessibility

- Export images at dimensions appropriate to their display size.
- Use modern image formats where supported and preserve a suitable fallback strategy.
- Avoid uploading huge originals directly into page layouts.
- Use responsive image sizing and lazy-load below-the-fold imagery where appropriate.
- Give informative images concise, contextual alt text.
- Do not repeat nearby text verbatim in alt text.
- Mark decorative imagery as decorative so assistive technology can skip it.

## 6. Brand and rights checks

Before an asset is committed or published, confirm:
- The business owns it or has permission to use it.
- The source and any licence terms are recorded.
- Required attribution is included.
- The asset contains no private customer data or confidential documents.
- The image does not include another brand's logo or protected campaign material without permission.

Do not copy images, text, logos, or page designs from Peter England, Allen Solly, or any other reference brand. Use references to discuss broad design qualities only.

## 7. Content approval

Record the person who approved product claims and the date they were checked. Recheck content when specifications, product availability, contact details, or business policies change.
